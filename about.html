About

DaxMac

DaxMac helps developer-facing companies uncover implementation risks before they become support and engineering work.

We uncover situations where customers can form a reasonable but incorrect understanding of how a product works — and make implementation decisions based on that understanding.

The investigation starts from the beginning of the developer journey: where a developer first discovers the product, creates an account, gets set up, and begins implementing it. We follow that journey as a first-time developer would, looking for the points where assumptions, friction, or unexpected implementation requirements can emerge.

For engagements that include live validation, we also go beyond the presented experience. We create an account, fund or credit it where required, use the live API or SDK, and work through the actual implementation path toward production. This lets us compare what a developer is led to expect with what they encounter when they actually implement the product.

The goal is to identify what a first-time developer can realistically encounter — particularly the problems that may not be obvious to the team that built the product.

The problem DaxMac addresses is the gap between how a product is designed to be implemented and how a customer can reasonably understand it should be implemented.

When that gap goes unnoticed, the resulting problems can surface through customer questions, implementation delays, support requests, engineering intervention, or problems further into the customer's integration.

DaxMac provides an independent investigation of that implementation experience, giving product and engineering teams visibility into implementation risks they may not see from inside the company.

Founded by

Goodness Nwajichukwu

I founded DaxMac around a simple observation: developer-facing products are built by people who already understand how they work. Their customers don't have that advantage.

My work focuses on looking at the product from the customer's side of implementation and uncovering where a reasonable interpretation can lead to the wrong implementation decision.

DaxMac turns those observations into evidence-based findings that teams can use to understand and address implementation risk.
