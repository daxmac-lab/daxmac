Where Integration Problems Begin

Developers make decisions based on what they understand about a product. Sometimes a reasonable assumption about how the product behaves leads to an implementation that does not match reality.

The problem may not surface until the integration reaches an edge case, production environment, asynchronous workflow, failure condition, or another part of the product lifecycle.

By then, the company may be dealing with support questions, engineering intervention, failed implementations, customer frustration, or production incidents.

DaxMac investigates those assumptions before they become someone else's production problem.

How We Find the Problem

Every DaxMac review starts with one question: What can a reasonable developer believe from this experience?

01. Experience

Examine what the developer encounters while moving through setup, integration, and production-facing flows.

02. Assumption

Identify the mental model a competent developer can naturally form, including what they believe the product will handle for them.

03. Product Reality

Examine what the available product and technical evidence implies about what the developer will actually encounter and what responsibility remains with them.

04. Consequence

Identify where the assumption can meet reality and create implementation friction, production problems, trust issues, support burden, or other business consequences.
