Methodology

Every DaxMac audit follows the same production-first framework.

Documentation

Review the onboarding documentation and identify what the developer is explicitly told—and what they must infer.

Developer Assumption

Identify the mental model a competent developer can reasonably form from the documented flow.

Implementation Decision

Trace the assumption to the implementation decision it can reasonably cause a developer to make.

Production Trigger

Identify the condition under which the assumption can become consequential or cause the implementation to behave differently from what the developer expected.

Business Impact

Connect the resulting risk to reliability, developer trust, support burden, adoption, or revenue.
