Methodology

Every DaxMac audit starts with the same question: What can a developer reasonably believe from this experience?

01. Experience

Examine what the developer actually encounters while moving through onboarding, integration, and other production-facing flows.

02. Assumption

Identify the mental model a competent developer can naturally form from that experience, including what they believe the product will handle for them.

03. Product Reality

Examine what the available product and technical evidence implies about what the developer will actually encounter and what responsibility remains with them.

04. Consequence

Identify where the assumption can meet reality and create friction, implementation risk, production problems, trust issues, or other business consequences.

About DaxMac
