Developer Implementation Review · Area 04

Success Criteria

Voice AI Platform

Investigation question

How do success criteria determine what a simulation considers a successful outcome?

What we investigated

We traced how the platform converts a simulation's conversation and observable results into a pass or fail outcome, including structured outputs, comparators, expected values, and required versus optional criteria.

What we found

A simulation passes according to the specific conditions defined in its success criteria.

A passing result therefore establishes that the configured criteria were satisfied. It does not, by itself, establish that the complete real-world outcome occurred.

Why it matters

A criterion can evaluate what the assistant reported without verifying the underlying external system.

For example, the platform's appointment example explicitly distinguishes between reporting appointment details and verifying calendar state.

Implementation implication

Developers need to inspect what each required success criterion actually measures before treating a passing simulation as evidence that the underlying workflow succeeded.

What this demonstrates

DaxMac investigates what a product's testing results actually establish, including the boundary between a successful test result and the real-world outcome the developer intends to validate.
