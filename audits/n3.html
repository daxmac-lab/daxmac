Developer Implementation Review · Area 03

AI Tester Behavior

Voice AI Platform

Investigation question

How does the AI tester's behavior affect what a simulation actually exercises and validates?

What we investigated

We examined how the AI tester's personality, turn-taking behavior, interruptions, follow-up questions, speaking behavior, and other configurable behaviors affect the interaction with the assistant.

What we found

A simulation validates the assistant against the specific behavior configured for the AI tester.

Changing how the tester behaves can change the interaction being exercised, even when the assistant and scenario remain the same.

A passing simulation therefore does not establish how the assistant will behave with caller behaviors that the configured tester never exercises.

Why it matters

A highly cooperative tester may allow an assistant to complete a workflow without exercising interruptions, unclear responses, follow-up questions, pushback, or other conversational conditions.

Those behaviors need to be represented in the test if the developer wants the simulation to validate them.

Implementation implication

Developers need to understand what caller behavior each AI tester configuration actually exercises before treating a simulation result as evidence about broader caller interactions.

Evidence

A controlled comparison of tester behavior can show whether changing the tester's behavior changes the interaction exercised by the same assistant and scenario.

Result

The simulation result is conditional on the AI tester behavior used to produce it.

What this demonstrates

DaxMac investigates what a product's testing and implementation surfaces actually establish, including the conditions under which a successful result is valid.

Confidentiality
